About QuickBite

QuickBite is built around one idea: great fast food should be quick to order and easy to track. Browse the menu, choose delivery or collection, and follow your order from the kitchen to your door.

Our menu covers burgers, pizza, chicken, wraps and tacos, snacks, desserts, drinks and milkshakes. Prices are in South African Rand, and what you see on the menu is what you pay.

Questions or feedback? Visit our contact page.
